# Research status

No research novelty or publication result is claimed. The current contribution is
a reproducible engineering implementation and test artifact.

Candidate future study: bounded adaptive batching under bursty offered load,
optimizing CPU cost subject to a declared tail-latency objective. First review
current literature, implement strong fixed-size/deadline baselines, separate
training/tuning workloads from evaluation, and define independent repeated trials.
The existing offline repeat-one-frame harness is insufficient to evaluate that
hypothesis. Negative results must remain reportable.

A future report should contain motivation, related work, support boundaries,
architecture, hypotheses, implementation, methodology, raw results, threats to
validity, limitations and artifact instructions. A paper is conditional on evidence.
